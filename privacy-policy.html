Privacy Policy for Samay ESS

Application: Samay ESS (com.paramount.samay) Developer: Paramount Office Systems Private Ltd Last Updated: November 2026

Paramount Office Systems Private Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Samay ESS (Employee Self-Service) mobile application (Package ID: com.paramount.samay). This Privacy Policy explains in detail our practices regarding the collection, processing, and stringent protection of employee personal information, specifically photographic biometric data and geographic location coordinates.

Our Absolute Data Protection Guarantee

Your data is fully protected and NEVER sold, rented, leased, monetized, or shared with third-party advertisers, marketing agencies, or data brokers under any circumstances. All data collected is used solely by your employer for legitimate workplace attendance, shift scheduling, and HR/payroll administration.

1. Photographic & Facial Biometric Data (Camera Access)

Samay ESS requests access to the device camera strictly when an employee initiates an attendance clock-in or clock-out action. Here is why and how photo data is processed:

A. Contactless Biometric Punch Verification

  • Preventing Proxy Attendance: A selfie snapshot is captured at the moment of clock-in and clock-out to verify that the authorized employee is personally present, preventing fraudulent clock-ins or "buddy punching".
  • Template Matching: The face captured during the punch is matched against the employee's official reference template approved by the organization's HR.

B. Active Anti-Spoofing & Liveness Detection

  • The app runs real-time anti-spoof checks on-device to verify physical human presence, rejecting printed photos, screen replays, and digital masks.

C. Tamper-Proof Attendance Receipt

  • The captured punch photo forms an immutable, time-stamped attendance receipt for employee protection and transparent audit records.
  • Scope Limitation: The camera is NEVER accessed in the background or when the app is idle. Captures only happen on explicit user tap of "Mark Attendance".

2. Location Information & Radial Geofencing (GPS Access)

Samay ESS accesses device location strictly at the exact moment of marking attendance. Here is how geographic data is used:

A. Proof of Attendance Location

  • When an employee submits an attendance punch, the app records the GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) and resolves the physical office/field address.
  • This provides verified proof of where duty commenced or concluded for office staff, on-site personnel, and field service teams.

B. Radial Geofencing Boundary Enforcement (Present & Future Upgrades)

  • Designated Work Zones: Employers configure authorized circular geographic zones (geofences) around headquarters, branch offices, client project sites, or operational hubs.
  • Radius Verification: The application calculates the radial distance from the employee's current coordinates to the designated office anchor point. Punches are validated when the employee is physically within the authorized radius (e.g., within 50m, 100m, or customized boundary of the workplace).
  • Out-of-boundary punches are transparently flagged as field or remote duty, subject to managerial approval.

C. Strict No-Background-Tracking Policy

  • Zero Passive Surveillance: Samay ESS does NOT track employee movements continuously. Location is accessed exclusively for a few seconds during the active punch submission. When the app is closed or in the background, location services are completely inactive.

3. Personal & Employment Information We Collect

To enable self-service workflows, the application processes:

  • Employee Profiles: Employee ID, Full Name, Work Email, Phone Number, and Department.
  • Attendance Logs: Clock-in/Clock-out timestamps, shift rosters, punch receipts, and regularisation requests.
  • Leave Management: Leave applications, leave balances, approval history, and compensatory off records.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and network state solely for crash diagnostics and technical stability.

5. Enterprise Data Security & Encryption

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to protect employee data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and corporate servers use TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Biometric embeddings and attendance logs stored on databases are secured with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Hardware-Backed Keystore: User authentication tokens on mobile devices are protected inside the Android Keystore.

6. Zero Monetization & Third-Party Disclosure Policy

  • We do NOT sell, trade, or rent employee personal information, photos, or location records to any third party.
  • We do NOT integrate advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or third-party marketing brokers.
  • Data is strictly accessible only to authorized HR and Payroll personnel within the employee's sponsoring organization.

7. Retention & Employee Rights

  • Retention: Attendance records are retained in compliance with corporate HR policies and statutory labor laws.
  • Access & Correction: Employees can review their punch logs directly in the app and request corrections via the "Miss Punch" regularization feature.
  • Account Deletion: Upon separation or departure from the organization, account access is terminated and data archived per statutory retention mandates.

8. Children's Privacy

Samay ESS is strictly an enterprise corporate workplace tool and is not intended for or directed to individuals under the age of 18.
